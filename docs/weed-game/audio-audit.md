## Mixing policy (implemented in src/audio.js)

- Buses: music (per-track trim → duck → Music slider), ambience (duck → Music slider × 0.9), sfx categories (move / combat / boss / env / cine) → Effects slider, ui → Effects slider, voice (compressor → Voice slider). All buses → master limiter (DynamicsCompressorNode, threshold -6 dB, ratio 20, fast attack) → output.
- Targets: music -18..-20 LUFS, voice -14..-16 LUFS, sfx -16..-12 LUFS momentary (impacts loudest, foley/UI quietest), ambience -24..-28 LUFS.
- Ducking groups: speech ducks music to 0.32 (characters) / 0.16 (narrator) and ambience to 0.55 / 0.4; big impacts (events with `duck` in sfx_map.js) duck music and, if marked `ambDuck`, ambience. Kaibutsu roars duck less (0.55).
